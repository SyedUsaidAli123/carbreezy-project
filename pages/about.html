Our Story

Pakistan's Trusted Car Marketplace

CarBreezy started in 2015 with a simple vision: to make car buying and selling in Pakistan as easy, transparent, and trustworthy as possible. What began as a small team of car enthusiasts has grown into Pakistan's leading automotive digital marketplace.

Today, we connect thousands of buyers and sellers across Pakistan every day. From Karachi to Islamabad, from Peshawar to Quetta — CarBreezy is helping Pakistanis find their dream cars with confidence.
